      Settings for total control
      • USB debugging: Settings → About phone → Software information → tap Build number 7 times, then Settings → Developer options → USB debugging on.
      • Allow this computer: on "Allow USB debugging?" tick Always allow from this computer, then Allow.
      • Samsung Auto Blocker off: Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → Off (otherwise taps never arrive).
      • Stay awake: Developer options → Stay awake on, so the screen stays on while it charges.
      • Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO: also Developer options → USB debugging (Security settings) on.
      • Leave it unlocked: Falcor never unlocks your phone.

                                The Lay of the Keeper

                                Before the first screen ever glowed, the old dragons kept the lore of play. From the high places they watched every game ever made, and learned its hidden paths: where the treasure lies buried, which doors are snares, how a level is won by patience and never by haste. One by one the old dragons went to their long sleep, until only the eldest remained.

                                Falcor would not sleep. He came down from the mountain with everything his kin had known, and he built a hall to keep it. This is that hall.

                                Here the Keeper watches over the phone you bring him. Through its glass he sees what you see, and through its glowing frame he learns. When you rest, he plays on through the long night, and when the sun returns, the spoils of the day wait for you, counted and true.

                                Nothing here is learned by chance. When you play before him, he marks every tap, every pause and every word you type, and weaves them into runs that answer to you alone. A run once shown can be cast again, unchanged, as often as you call for it. Each game he plays leaves him wiser for the next, and the more you show him, the deeper his memory runs.

                                The old laws still bind him. He never spends what is not his: no purchase, no wager, not a single coin of yours leaves your hand by his claw. Nothing in this hall stirs until you have blessed it. And should you speak the word of stilling, stop, every wing in the hall folds at once.

                                The hall keeps its own secrets. Your keys rest here and nowhere else, each one whispered only to its own keeper. No website peers through these windows, and nothing that passes between you and the dragon is ever sold.

                                This knowledge is not written in any book. It passes only from master to apprentice, from hand to claw, and you are now one of the very few who hold it.

                                Few are ever shown the way in. You were. Guard what you learn here, and teach him well.— Falcor, Keeper of the Hub

                                Below the Hall

                                A few leaves from the Keeper's private ledger.

                                The first light in the workshop

                                Before the Hub had windows, it was a workbench: one phone, one careful hand, and a dragon learning the difference between a button that opens a door and one that asks too much. The first rule was written before the first run: the hand chooses; the Keeper follows.

                                So the hall was built with a place for every kind of memory. A screen may be observed, but a secret stays veiled. A lesson may be kept, but only because its maker asked. A sequence may be repeated, but never beyond the owner's word. These are not locks on the door. They are the shape of the door itself.

                                When the phone is near, the Keeper can watch its glass and carry out a run. When it is far, the hall waits without pretending it can reach. The Library remembers what you made; the Sandbox is where you teach it; and the red word stop is never more than one call away.

                                Every new block is another mark in the margin. Some are small: a pause, a tap, a name typed carefully. Together they become a path that belongs to you, held here on your computer and ready when you are.

                                The archive keeps no prophecy. Only what you chose to teach.— from the Keeper's ledger, undated

                                Filed under: “Do not wake”

                                There is no sleeping dragon beneath the floor. There is, however, a very old button that says Stop. The Keeper insists this is a much better arrangement.
